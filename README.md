#  Day 19 – Top 10 Products by Sales

##  Overview

This project was completed as part of my Data Analytics Internship at Veda Technology.

The objective of this task is to identify and analyze the **Top 10 Products by Sales** using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The analysis was performed using both **Microsoft Excel** and **Python** to calculate product-wise total sales, rank the products, check for ties, and visualize the results.


##  Objectives

- Identify the Top 10 products based on total sales.
- Calculate product-wise total sales.
- Rank products in descending order of sales.
- Check for ties at the Top 10 cutoff.
- Visualize the Top 10 products using a bar chart.
- Compare and validate the results using Excel and Python.



##  Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab
- Microsoft Excel
- GitHub



##  Dataset

The analysis uses the **Sample Superstore dataset**, which contains order-level information including:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer information
- Product ID
- Product Name
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit
- Regional information



##  Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Loading
The Excel dataset was loaded into Python using Pandas.

### 2. Data Inspection
The dataset was examined to understand:

- Number of rows and columns
- Column names
- Data types
- Missing values
- Dataset structure

### 3. Product-wise Sales Analysis
Sales were grouped by **Product Name** and summed to calculate the total sales generated by each product.

### 4. Ranking
Products were sorted in descending order based on total sales.

### 5. Top 10 Identification
The first 10 products with the highest total sales were selected.

### 6. Tie Check
The 10th-ranked and 11th-ranked products were compared to check for a tie at the Top 10 cutoff.

### 7. Visualization
A horizontal bar chart was created using Matplotlib to compare the sales performance of the Top 10 products.



##  Key Findings

- The analysis successfully identified the **Top 10 products by total sales**.
- **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** recorded the highest total sales of approximately **61,599.82**.
- The Top 10 products generated combined sales of approximately **244,620.20**.
- Products were ranked in descending order based on total sales.
- The 10th-ranked product recorded sales of approximately **17,030.31**.
- The 11th-ranked product recorded sales of approximately **16,829.90**.
- No tie was identified at the Top 10 cutoff.



## Visualization

A horizontal bar chart was created to clearly compare the total sales of the Top 10 products.

The horizontal format was chosen because product names are relatively long and are easier to read this way.
